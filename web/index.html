Game files

Import your NFL 2K5 disc: an Xbox disc image (.iso / .xiso) or an extracted disc folder containing default.xbe and vc_53450030.

The import copies the disc (several GB) into the browser's private storage once. Saves and settings are kept there too, apart from the disc.

Play

Click the game to give it the keyboard. Controllers connect when you press one of their buttons. The web build boots as far as the runtime goes today; see docs/WEB.md.
